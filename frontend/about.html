Who we are

A school for every stage of early learning

Hello Kidz School welcomes children from preschool through Class 5. We are a community of families and educators who believe the early years shape how children learn and how they feel about themselves for life.

Child development

Foundational learning for the whole child

We support more than academic skills. These are the areas we care about in the early years.

Language and communication

Listening, speaking and early reading readiness.

Thinking and problem-solving

Asking questions, noticing patterns and trying different ways to solve a problem.

Social and emotional growth

Making friends, sharing, taking turns and understanding feelings.

Physical and motor skills

Balance, coordination and the hand control needed for writing and drawing.

Creativity and expression

Art, music, movement and make-believe that help children express themselves.

Confidence and independence

Taking on small responsibilities and feeling capable.
