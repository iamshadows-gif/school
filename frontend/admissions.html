FAQs

Admissions questions

General answers. Where a detail is still being confirmed by the school, we say so.

Which classes can I enquire about?

You can enquire about Preschool and Classes 1 to 5. Seat availability is confirmed by the school.

When does admission open?

Admission dates are pending school confirmation. Please contact the school for the latest information.

What are the fees?

Fee information is not published on this website yet. The school will share fee details when they are confirmed.

Does sending an enquiry guarantee a seat?

No. An enquiry is the first step only. Admission depends on the school's confirmed process and availability.

Which documents will I need?

The school will share the document list when it is confirmed. Please do not upload or send identity documents such as Aadhaar or APAAR details through this website.

Can I visit the school before applying?

Please ask us through the enquiry form or by contacting the school. Visit arrangements are pending school confirmation.

How will the school contact me?

The school will use the contact number and details you provide in your enquiry. Contact timing is to be confirmed.
