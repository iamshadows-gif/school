General learning areas

Areas of learning we cover

These are general areas of learning. The school's subject list and timetable will be confirmed separately.

Language and communication

Listening, speaking, reading readiness and writing.

Numeracy

Counting, patterns, shapes and early mathematical thinking.

Environment and exploration

Curiosity about living things, materials and the world around us.

Art and creative expression

Drawing, colour, craft and imaginative work.

Music and movement

Songs, rhythm and movement that build listening and coordination.

Physical development and play

Active play, balance and teamwork.

Learning through activities

Skills we develop

Children build these skills through everyday classroom life and planned activities. Learn more on our activities page.

Communication

Expressing ideas, listening carefully and speaking confidently in groups.

Creativity

Trying new ideas, making things and expressing feelings through art, music and stories.

Problem-solving

Observing, asking questions, trying solutions and learning from mistakes.
